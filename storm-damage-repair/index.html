Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in The Hammocks

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
